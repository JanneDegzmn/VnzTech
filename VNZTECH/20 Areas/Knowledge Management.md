# Knowledge Management

## Purpose

May mapagkakatiwalaang lugar para sa ideas, sources, decisions, at next actions na madaling balikan.

## Suggested habits

- **Araw-araw, 5 minuto:** capture, process ng ilang notes, piliin ang next action, at itala ang learning.
- **Bawat linggo, 20 minuto:** review ng inbox, projects, responsibilities, at priorities.

## Standards

- May source at context ang importanteng claims.
- May konkretong next action ang active projects.
- Naka-link ang related notes kapag may malinaw na ugnayan.
- Malinaw kung suggestion, commitment, o unverified finding ang nakasulat.
