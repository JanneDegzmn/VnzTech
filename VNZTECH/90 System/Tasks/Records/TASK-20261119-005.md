## Read-only findings to reuse

- Session completion currently has four relevant paths: `backend/login.php`, `backend/verify_seller_2fa.php`, `backend/social_auth_service.php` (`vnzSocialAuthCompleteLogin`) and `backend/remember_login.php`. Enforcing a fresh challenge in only password login would leave other routes uncovered. Any required challenge needs a shared trusted completion boundary, clear remembered-session policy, recovery, and preservation of required TOTP.
- Ordinary camera similarity alone is not adequate login proof. Client `verified=true` or a supplied descriptor is bypassable; trusted processing, spoof resistance and another proof would need evaluation. Existing ID-review approval does not itself authorize every future login.
- [WebAuthn](https://www.w3.org/TR/webauthn-3/) and [Apple passkeys](https://developer.apple.com/passkeys/) are cross-device candidates, not Windows-only. Device verification can use biometrics or PIN; it does not guarantee face-only authentication or compare against the submitted ID. Owner has not selected this approach. [OWASP MFA guidance](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/Multifactor_Authentication_Cheat_Sheet.html) informed the research.
- PHP 8.2.12/OpenSSL/mbstring were observed; [lbuchs/WebAuthn](https://github.com/lbuchs/WebAuthn) was inspected only as a future candidate. No package installed, feature tested, authentication policy changed or login enrollment performed.

ID evidence retention from task 004 must not silently become indefinite biometric retention for this future task. Revisit purpose, consent, enrollment/storage and recovery in its separate proposal. No user images/descriptors/secrets in task notes.
